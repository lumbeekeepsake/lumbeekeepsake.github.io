Privacy Policy — Lumbee Keepsake

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Lumbee Keepsake ("the app") is a free, independent, volunteer-run educational and cultural preservation project. This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or share your personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no login, and no sign-up. It does not ask for, collect, or transmit your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking software.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few small things on your phone so it works the way you left it: your "Preservation Streak" (how many days in a row you've completed a quiz), whether you chose the light or dark theme, which map style you picked, and a copy of the community places list so the map works offline. These stay on your phone, are never sent anywhere, and are removed if you uninstall the app.

Content the app downloads

The Photo Album and the community-suggested places on the Interactive Map and Local Directory are loaded from a public GitHub repository. On Android, the map itself is drawn by Google Maps; on iPhone, by Apple Maps. Like any internet request, these contact the provider's servers, which may log standard technical information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. The app itself does not collect or keep this information.

Photo submissions

Submitting a photo is optional and happens outside the app, through a Google Form. You may add a caption and your name; if you do, they are shown with the photo in the app, and you can leave them blank to stay anonymous. Every submission is reviewed by hand before it appears, and phone numbers are never published. Anything you submit through the form is also governed by Google's privacy policy. To have a photo removed later, use "Report this photo" in the app or email us (below).

Place suggestions

Suggesting a place is optional and also happens through a Google Form. If you drop a pin on the map, the form receives the location of that pin, which is the spot you chose, not your phone's location. The app never reads your phone's location. Suggestions are reviewed by hand, and only details about the place itself (its name, type, address, business phone number and description) are shown in the app. Your name, if you give it, is not shown.

Links to other services

The app links to services run by others, including Google Maps, Apple Maps and Waze (for directions), the Lumbee Tribe's official website, and Facebook. When you follow one of those links you leave the app, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

The app needs only internet access. It does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Community photo submissions must follow the app's guidelines, which prohibit photos of children without a parent or guardian's consent.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions, or want a photo removed? Email lumbeeheritageapp@gmail.com.
